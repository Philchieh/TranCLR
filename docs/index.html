TranCLR

Beyond Binary Contrast: Modeling Continuous Skeleton Action Spaces with Transitional Anchors

A transitional anchor-based contrastive learning framework for continuous, uncertainty-aware skeleton action representation.

Abstract

Self-supervised contrastive learning has emerged as a powerful paradigm for skeleton-based action recognition by enforcing consistency in the embedding space. However, existing methods rely on binary contrastive objectives that overlook the intrinsic continuity of human motion, resulting in fragmented feature clusters and rigid class boundaries.

TranCLR captures the continuous geometry of the action space through transitional anchors. The Action Transitional Anchor Construction (ATAC) explicitly models transitional states, while Multi-Level Geometric Manifold Calibration (MGMC) adaptively calibrates the action manifold across multiple levels of continuity.

Highlights

  • Models motion continuity beyond binary positive-negative contrast.
  • Builds transitional anchors for smoother skeleton representations.
  • Improves accuracy and calibration on NTU RGB+D, NTU RGB+D 120, and PKU-MMD.
